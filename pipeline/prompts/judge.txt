Each case below is one Fieldbook support conversation (personal data masked as [TYPE]) followed by the claim verdicts that code has already checked against Fieldbook's records, and the handoff record if one exists.
Use the verdicts as given. Do not re-check facts. For every case return:
1. resolution: "no" only when the customer is left with no usable answer or next step: the question goes unanswered, the assistant loops, a request for a person is refused or ignored, the reply repeats a step the customer said already failed, or every answer and step in the reply failed its check or is unsafe. If the reply gives at least one step or path that passed its check (or was not checked) and lets the customer get, check or act on what they asked about, resolution is "yes", even when a stated amount, date, window or status failed. Wrong facts are scored by check 1, not here. Give message_id and a one-line reason.
2. tone: "problem" only if the wording of an assistant message is sarcastic, blames the customer, or brushes off their concern (for example "that is not our problem" or "calm down"). Sending the customer to the wrong place or missing a handoff is scored elsewhere, not as tone. Otherwise "ok". Give message_id and a one-line reason.
3. handoff_context: read the HANDOFF RECORD listed after the conversation. If it lists a handoff, answer "covers" when its context tells the person receiving it what the customer needs (and what was already tried, if anything was), or "missing" when the context is empty or describes a different issue. Answer "not_applicable" only when the HANDOFF RECORD says none.
